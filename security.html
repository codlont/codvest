Security & Privacy

Security is part of every decision.

Protecting accounts, personal information and investment-related activities is an important part of the CodVest platform vision. We aim to build with transparency, responsibility and security awareness at the centre.

Protect Your Account

Your security also depends on good habits.

Users should take care when accessing cryptocurrency services. No legitimate platform representative should ask you to reveal your private wallet recovery phrase or password.

  • Use a strong, unique account password.
  • Enable additional account verification when available.
  • Never share your password or recovery phrase.
  • Check website addresses before signing in.
  • Be cautious of unexpected messages and suspicious links.
  • Contact official support if you notice unusual activity.

Privacy matters

CodVest intends to explain what information is collected, why it is needed, how it is used and how users can exercise applicable privacy rights.

Personal information should not be treated as a product. Access should be limited to authorised purposes and handled under applicable data protection requirements.

Detailed data handling practices will be set out in the platform's Privacy Policy before live registration begins.

Investment Awareness

Understand cryptocurrency risks

Security is not the same as investment protection. Cryptocurrency markets carry financial risks even when accounts and systems are properly protected.

Important Risk Disclosure

Cryptocurrency prices can be highly volatile. You may lose some or all of your invested capital. Digital assets may also involve custody, technology, liquidity, operational and regulatory risks. No investment return is guaranteed. Users should review all applicable terms and seek independent professional advice where appropriate before making investment decisions.

Service Availability and Regulatory Notice

The security features described on this page represent the platform's intended approach and are not a certification, audit report or guarantee that every control is currently operational. Live investment services will be offered only when the required legal, regulatory, operational and security arrangements are in place.
