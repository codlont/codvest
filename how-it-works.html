Your journey

Four steps to get started.

The following outlines the intended customer journey. Access to investment services will depend on eligibility, verification, applicable approvals and the availability of live services.

01

Create your account

Start by providing the information requested during registration. Use accurate details and create secure account credentials. Registration alone does not mean you are approved to invest.

ACCOUNT REGISTRATION
02

Complete identity verification

Complete the applicable identity, eligibility and compliance checks. Additional information may be requested before an account can access investment services.

IDENTITY AND ELIGIBILITY
03

Review investment options

Review the available investment arrangements, minimum amounts, fees, holding periods, withdrawal conditions and associated risks. Make sure you understand the terms before deciding whether to proceed.

INVESTMENT INFORMATION
04

Monitor your portfolio

Where live investment services are available, use your account to review recorded deposits, withdrawals, investment positions and other relevant account information.

Before you invest

Make informed decisions.

Cryptocurrency is a high-risk asset class. Prices can change rapidly, markets can become illiquid and losses can occur. Take time to understand the investment and its conditions before committing money.

✓

Read all investment terms and conditions.

✓

Understand fees, withdrawal rules and applicable timeframes.

✓

Understand the possibility of losing your invested capital.

✓

Only use official CodVest communication and account channels.

Important investment notice

No investment return is guaranteed. Cryptocurrency investments can lose value, including the possibility of losing the entire amount invested. Past performance does not predict future results.

Do not deposit funds until the relevant investment service, its legal status, custody arrangements, fees and withdrawal conditions have been clearly explained and independently verified.
