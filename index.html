QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is CodVest?

CodVest is a proposed cryptocurrency investment management platform being developed by CODLONT.

How does managed investing work?

Under a managed account arrangement, an appropriately authorised investment manager manages the agreed portfolio within the customer's mandate.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Cryptocurrency markets are volatile. Investments can lose value, and customers may lose some or all of their invested capital.

Can I withdraw my investment?

Withdrawal rights, processing periods and any applicable restrictions will be clearly disclosed in the final investment agreement.

When will CodVest launch?

The platform is currently in development. Live investment services will only be offered after the necessary regulatory and operational arrangements are established.
